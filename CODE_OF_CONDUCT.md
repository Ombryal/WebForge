# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be respectful, be constructive, and assume good faith. Disagreements about
code, design, and direction are normal and welcome, personal attacks,
harassment, and discrimination are not.

## Expected behavior

- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Accept that reasonable people can disagree about language design.
- Be patient with beginners and with questions that have been asked before.
- Respect that this is a small, early-stage project maintained in spare
  time, reviews and responses may take a while.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests, or
  any other project space.
- Discriminatory jokes or language.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Anything else that would make a reasonable person feel unwelcome or
  unsafe participating.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces, issues, pull requests, discussions,
and commit messages, and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Given the project's size, moderation is at the maintainer's discretion:
this can mean anything from a request to rephrase something, to editing or
removing content, to blocking someone from further participation, depending
on severity. If you experience or witness behavior that violates this, you
can raise it with the maintainer directly through GitHub.
